The format

Heads up — the bracket crosses here

After the quarter-finals, winners do not stay on the same side of the screen. That is normal — it is not a bug.

  • What you will see: a quarter-final winner on the left can show up in a semi-final or final in the center, paired with a team that came from the right side.
  • Why FIFA does it: so teams from the same original side of the draw cannot meet again until the final (if both keep winning).
  • How to pick: click a team to advance; click again to undo. Clearing an early match also clears later rounds that depended on it.
