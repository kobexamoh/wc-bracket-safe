How this bracket works

New to the World Cup format? Here's the 60-second version. Already know it? Just close this and start ranking.

The format

Each group has four teams that all play each other once. You're predicting how each group will finish — 1st through 4th.

Who advances

The top two in every group advance. This year there's also room for the 8 best 3rd-place teams across all groups, so your 3rd-vs-4th call can matter once the knockouts are set.

How to rank

Click teams in your predicted order: the first click is 1st place, the next is 2nd, and so on. Click a ranked team again to clear it — the teams below shift up to fill the gap. Submit once every group has at least its top two.

Want a redo? Each group has its own Clear button, or use Deselect all to reset every group at once.
